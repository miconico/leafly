What this series explores
📋
Digital vs Physical
Every feature in Leafly is benchmarked against its physical equivalent — sticky notes, corkboards, index cards — to understand what each medium does best.
🧠
The Science of Lists
Interviews with psychologists and productivity researchers on why writing things down works, and how digital tools can replicate or surpass the effect.
✏️
reMarkable & E Ink
Investigating how E Ink devices like the reMarkable tablet can bridge the gap — combining the feel of handwriting with the power of digital organisation.
🔄
Iterative Building
The messy, honest process of building in public — whiteboard sketches, failed approaches, prototype pivots, and the decisions that shaped the final product.
